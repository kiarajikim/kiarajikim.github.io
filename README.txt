KIARA (JIHYUN) KIM — PERSONAL WEBSITE
=====================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html      Home page
  cv.html         CV page (shows your CV PDF)
  research.html   Research page
  teaching.html   Teaching page
  style.css       Design (colors, fonts, layout) for all pages
  images/         Put your profile photo here
  files/          Put your PDFs here


STEP 1 — ADD YOUR PHOTO AND PDFs
  Name them EXACTLY like this (lowercase, same spelling):

  images/profile.jpg                  Your profile photo
  files/CV_2026_Kiara.pdf             Your CV
  files/buyer-initiated-auctions.pdf  Job market paper

  How to get them:
  - Photo: open your Google Site, right-click the photo,
    "Save image as...", rename to profile.jpg.
    (If it's a .png, rename it profile.png and change
    "profile.jpg" to "profile.png" in index.html.)
  - PDFs: open each one in Google Drive, File > Download.


STEP 2 — CREATE THE GITHUB REPOSITORY
  1. Sign in at github.com (create a free account if needed).
  2. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository".
  3. Repository name: yourusername.github.io
     (use YOUR GitHub username, e.g. kiarakim.github.io)
  4. Set it to Public. Click "Create repository".


STEP 3 — UPLOAD THE FILES
  1. In the new repository, click "uploading an existing file"
     (or Add file > Upload files).
  2. Open this kiara-site folder on your computer, select
     EVERYTHING INSIDE it (not the folder itself), and drag
     it into the browser. The images/ and files/ folders
     will upload with their contents.
  3. Click "Commit changes".


STEP 4 — TURN ON GITHUB PAGES
  1. In the repository, go to Settings > Pages.
  2. Under "Build and deployment", Source: "Deploy from a branch".
  3. Branch: main, folder: / (root). Click Save.
  4. Wait 1–3 minutes, then visit:
     https://yourusername.github.io


UPDATING THE SITE LATER
  - To change text: open the .html file on GitHub, click the
    pencil icon, edit, and "Commit changes". The live site
    updates within a minute or two.
  - To replace a PDF (e.g. a new CV): upload a file with the
    SAME name into files/. It replaces the old one.
  - If you don't see changes, hard-refresh your browser
    (Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).


AFTER IT'S LIVE
  - Update the link on your CV, email signature, and job
    market profiles to the new address.
  - You can keep the Google Site up during the switch, or
    unpublish it once the GitHub site is working.
